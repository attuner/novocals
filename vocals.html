How Acapella Extraction Works

In professional stereo mixes, lead vocal tracks are almost universally recorded and placed in the stereo center ($L = R$), whereas wide instruments (electric guitars, synth pads, cymbals, reverbs, brass) are panned hard left or right.

• Mid Channel (Center): $M = (L + R) / 2$
• Side Channel (Stereo): $S = (L - R) / 2$
• Vocal Isolation Signal: $V_{iso} = M - (\text{Suppression} \times S)$

Bandpass & Presence Equalization: Even with side subtraction, center-panned instruments like kick drums, sub bass, and cymbals share the mid channel. Our DSP applies:

  • High-Pass Filter: Strips sub-frequencies below 160Hz (removing kick drum thuds & bass lines).
  • Low-Pass Filter: Cuts frequencies above 5.5kHz (stripping bright hi-hat sizzle & cymbal sheen).
  • Vocal Presence Peaking EQ: Boosts the vocal formant sweet-spot (1kHz - 3.5kHz) to bring human vocals forward.
