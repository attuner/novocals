Center-Channel Cancellation DSP

In modern commercial stereo recordings, lead vocals, kick drums, and bass lines are traditionally panned dead-center, meaning their waveform is identical in both Left (L) and Right (R) channels.

Vocal Removal Logic: By inverting one channel's phase and summing them (L - R), anything identical in both channels cancels out to zero, leaving stereo instruments (guitars, reverb, pads, synthesizers).

Bass Preservation: Because kick drums and bass guitars are also centered, pure phase subtraction destroys low frequencies. Our DSP engine splits the signal via a crossover filter: frequencies below 200Hz are kept in pristine mono and mixed back into the final instrumental!

💡 Pro Tip: Works best with high-quality stereo files where vocals are strictly mixed to center. Mono files or vintage 60s hard-panned tracks will not cancel properly.
